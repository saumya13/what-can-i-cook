# Creamy Mint Paneer (Pudina Shahi Paneer)

This rich, restaurant-style curry features soft cubes of paneer simmered in a velvety tomato-cashew gravy, finished with a luscious swirl of fresh cream and a bright, aromatic hint of fresh mint.

---

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cook Time:** 20 minutes
- **Servings:** 3–4

---

## Ingredients

### From Your Kitchen:

- **Paneer:** 250g (approx. 9 oz), cut into bite-sized cubes
- **Fresh Cream:** 3 tbsp
- **Cashews:** 10–12 whole cashews
- **Tomatoes:** 3 medium, roughly chopped

## Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Gravy Base

1. Heat **1 tbsp of oil or butter** in a pan over medium heat.
2. Add the sliced **onions** and sauté for 3–4 minutes.
3. Add the chopped **tomatoes** and **cashews**.
